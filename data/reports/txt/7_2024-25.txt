                           School Vision & Mission
The vision and mission of Po Leung Kuk No.1 W.H. Cheung College center around the
provision of exceptional and comprehensive education which cultivates academic excellence,
moral virtues, and personal development among students. Renowned for guiding students
towards personal and academic brilliance, our school constantly strives to create a holistic
learning environment.

Through the dedicated efforts of our staff and effective communication between the school
and parents, our institution has emerged as one of the most sought-after secondary schools in
Wong Tai Sin. With a clear and well-defined mission, we have been fostering a harmonious
working relationship and dedicated team spirit among the school community.

Our curriculum places equal emphasis on the five fundamental Chinese virtues: morals,
intelligence, physical development, sociability, and aesthetics. By nurturing our students with
these aspects, we aim to unlock the full potential of our young talent. Moreover, we are
committed to continuously providing high-quality education and fostering a caring and
stimulating school life for our future leaders.

As a reputable secondary school, we always endeavour to provide our students with a well-
rounded educational experience focusing not only on academic excellence but also on the
development of correct moral values, personal growth, and a sense of social responsibility.
We are dedicated to maintaining our position as a leading secondary school by consistently
providing exceptional education and unleashing the potential of our students.

Our School

Established in 1971, Po Leung Kuk No.1 W.H. Cheung College is an aided secondary school
with English as the medium of instruction for nearly all classes and subjects. Our students
consistently excel in scholastic achievements in public examinations. Most of our students
achieve outstanding results, with many attaining Levels 5**, 5*, and 5 in many subjects.
Moreover, most of our graduates successfully gain admission to different famous universities
in Hong Kong. Many of our alumni have achieved remarkable success in their respective
careers.

While preparing students for the HKDSE Examination is a short-term goal, our focus extends
beyond academic perfection. We aim to provide a holistic education which fosters students’
critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving skills, and character development, ensuring their
long-term success and personal growth.

We strive to maintain a balance between academic studies and extracurricular activities by
offering a multitude of learning opportunities. Our commitment lies in creating a supportive
environment that facilitates the holistic growth and all-round development of our students,
earning us a reputation for guiding our young talent towards personal and academic excellence.

Achievements and Reflections on Major Concerns

Achievements

1.1 Foster positive values and cultivate an atmosphere of appreciation among students
Students nurtured personal growth through a wide range of academic and non-academic
activities, from classroom tasks and after-school programmes to external competitions. These
experiences allowed them to explore their strengths and weaknesses, supported by
assessments, peer reviews, and feedback. Teachers collected student works to monitor
progress, while more able students were given extended tasks to deepen learning. Talent pools
were set up to identify and support gifted individuals, and evidence of growth could be seen
in awards, improved performance, and stronger peer support networks.


To sustain values education, the school promoted the 12 priority values with emphasis on
national identity, appreciation, harmony, and respect. National education was integrated
across subjects, enabling students to better understand the history and recent developments of
Hong Kong and mainland China, as well as key aspects of national security. Activities such
as the Chinese face-changing performance, the Chinese film appreciation, traditional food
tasting, the Chinese artwork creation, and Chinese music appreciation enriched cultural
learning, while flag-raising ceremonies strengthened the patriotic spirit. The latest APASO-
III results showed that our school ranked within the top 15% of Hong Kong schools in the
domain of national identity, reflecting the sustained impact of the school’s ongoing efforts in
national education. The Wishing Tree, jointly organised by the library and the Parent-Teacher
Association, also provided a meaningful platform where parents and students shared wishes
together, encouraging appreciation and deeper mutual understanding.

1.2 Building up a healthy lifestyle among students
During class periods, teachers dedicated time to guide discussions on emotional well-being
and teach strategies for managing stress, helping students build resilience in facing setbacks.
Morning assemblies and sharing sessions promoted a caring school culture, reinforcing
positive values and reminding students of the importance of taking time for relaxation and
ensuring adequate rest through effective time management. Group discussions, class based
activities led by different departments, along with study tours, further strengthened their social
bonds and enhanced their sense of relationship with peers and teachers. Many students
expressed strong agreement that their inter-personal relationships had improved and that they
had acquired effective conflict-resolution skills. Parent-student activities, including athletic
events and hands-on workshops, strengthened family relationships and provided opportunities
for students to practice teamwork and responsibility. The school implemented the 4Rs Mental
Health Charter through a whole-school approach, and the APASO-III results showed that
students’ p-scores in the affective domain more than doubled compared to that of the last

                                                5
school year, reflecting the discernible improvement in their mental well-being resulting from
these concerted efforts.
Students acquired knowledge through talks and lectures on information literacy, cyber safety,
diet, sleep hygiene, drug prevention, sex education, environmental protection, and financial
management, building a foundation for lifelong healthy practices. Professional development
organised by Po Leung Kuk enhanced teachers’ understanding of emerging risks, such as the
etomidate, equipping staff to better protect students. The library’s Book Café offered a
relaxing setting for reading and story-sharing, supporting well-being and fostering meaningful
peer interactions.

2.1 Strengthening students’ learning skills and habits in enhancing students’ learning
    efficacy
To improve learning effectiveness, the school adopted a wide range of strategies that fostered
both academic achievement and study discipline. Talks on The 7 Habits of Highly Effective
People inspired students to adopt a growth mindset, while summer enrichment classes in
Chinese, English, and Mathematics helped students consolidate knowledge before the
academic year began. Lesson observations led by the vice-principal and panel chairmen
created opportunities for professional exchange, ameliorating teaching practices and ensuring
students benefitted from improved classroom delivery.


Professional support was also extended to staff at all levels. New teachers, middle managers,
and janitors received induction and training that equipped them with the necessary skills and
mindset to better support students. Regular middle manager meetings further strengthened
leadership consistency, ensuring a more stable and encouraging learning environment.

2.2 Empowering students with new opportunities
Students were encouraged to pursue new challenges and express their talents through a range
of platforms. The busking area provided a lively stage for performances in music, drama, and
creative arts, boosting confidence and self-expression. Teachers integrated questioning
strategies, dynamic grouping, and e-learning tools into lessons, which enriched student
engagement and promoted critical thinking. Themed weeks also offered opportunities to link
learning with authentic contexts: STEAM Week fostered creativity and problem-solving,
while Chinese Week and English Week deepened cultural appreciation and language
development. Athletic and Aquatic Meets further cultivated perseverance, teamwork, and
personal growth beyond academics. The Student Language Ambassadors Programme was set
up to provide students with opportunities to make public announcements and speeches,
sharpening their language skills and building confidence in communication. The APASO-III
results showed that students’ p-score for sense of meaning in life more than doubled over the
previous year, while the p-score for satisfaction with school increased by nearly 40%. This


                                              6
highlights the strong positive impact of the school’s programmes on student growth and
engagement.

Support from alumni and the school’s sponsoring body expanded these opportunities even
further. A broad range of scholarships funded by alumni donors and external sponsors
recognised students’ outstanding performance, while staunch support from Po Leung Kuk
enabled the study trip to Beijing, broadening students’ horizons. Displays of student works
across the campus and school-wide events such as Parents’ Day celebrated accomplishments
and fostered a sense of pride and recognition.

2.3 Further raising awareness of catering for learner diversity
The school strengthened its commitment to learner diversity by expanding subject choices and
enriching inclusive platforms. Senior form students benefitted from the introduction of an
additional group of the Mathematics Extended Part Module 2 (M2), which provided advanced
learners with greater academic challenges. Robotics was incorporated into the teaching
curriculum, giving students hands-on experience in problem-solving and technological
innovation. These initiatives contributed to extraordinary results in STEAM competitions,
showcasing both talent and creativity.


The library continued to play a crucial role in fostering inclusion. Its Reading Oasis offered a
calm, welcoming space for mindfulness practices and casual reading. Cross-subject projects,
such as traditional Chinese attire appreciation and the collaboration of the Drama Society with
the Chinese Opera Club, broadened cultural horizons and encouraged interdisciplinary
learning among students. The busking area and opportunities such as the Student Language
Ambassadors Programme and many other programmes gave students authentic platforms to
demonstrate leadership and talents. Social welfare services further nurtured a sense of
responsibility and strengthened students’ commitment to community service.

2.4 Enhancing career guidance and life planning
Life planning education was integrated into classroom learning, and it was reinforced in junior
forms to better prepare students for future study and career planning. The Career Expo,
launched this year with participation from a wide range of distinguished alumni, provided
students with first-hand insight into various industries, helping them explore interests and
clarify aspirations. Alumni sharing sessions and personalised counselling further strengthened
students’ understanding of potential pathways and enhanced their confidence in career
planning.

Teacher capacity was strengthened through staff development workshops on JUPAS and life
planning counselling, equipping teachers with practical guidance skills. More than 90% of
participants reported that the training met their professional needs, and many expressed

                                               7
increased confidence in advising F.6 students on the programme selection. These initiatives
ensured that both teachers and students were better prepared to approach life planning and
career development with clarity and purpose.

Reflections

Reflecting on the year, promoting positive values and appreciation boosted student well being.
Expanded learning options supported academic and career growth, though further progress is
possible through added resources and strategic improvements.


To enhance the impact of form teacher periods on students’ well-being and interpersonal skills,
structured sessions will be introduced to strengthen class cultivation and emotional
development. Recognizing parents’ vital role, especially for new students, the new Parents’
Day will feature new activities, including the introduction to various support services, study
tips from panel heads, and a PTA-led sharing session. More case conferences will be held to
address individual needs and promote holistic well-being. Lesson observations will focus on
learner diversity and engaging classroom experiences. Academic performance will be boosted
through refined exam regulations, enhanced SEN support, and tailored supplementary classes.
Building on last year’s success, career guidance will be extended to include more industries
and greater involvement from students and parents.


To strengthen the implementation of values education, with a particular emphasis on national
education and national security, a larger board will be formed for the National Security
Education Working Group. This board will include teachers from various departments,
allowing for a broader exchange of ideas and the organization of more relevant activities to
reinforce these themes. The Library will take a more active role in promoting values education
and encouraging reading as a tool for learning. Increased collaboration between the Library
and different departments will be pursued, along with efforts to expand the collection by
acquiring additional printed books and electronic resources.

Our Learning and Teaching

The school used English as the medium of instruction in all subjects except for Chinese
Language, Chinese Literature, Chinese History, Putonghua, and Citizenship & Social
Development. The school conducted after-school, Saturday, and summer tutorial classes for
weaker students to cater for their learning needs. To be better in line with the Key Learning
Areas, the school grouped Economics, History, Geography, Chinese History, Citizenship and
Social Development, Liberal Studies, and Life and Society into Personal, Social and
Humanities Education, and Biology, Physics, Chemistry and Integrated Science into Science
Education. The school also often reviewed the elective options offered to senior form students
to cater for their interests.

Moral, Civic and National Education

Our school aimed to nurture in students the twelve priority values and attitudes, namely
perseverance, respect for others, responsibility, national identity, commitment, integrity,
benevolence, law-abidingness, empathy, diligence, filial piety and unity through
comprehensive and diversified learning experiences. A wide array of activities was organized,
such as morning sharing sessions, assemblies, class periods, community services, inter-class
noticeboard competitions, Cleanliness Weeks, F.1 and F.2 story-telling competitions, F.3
Drama performances, F.4 short speeches and F.4 and F.5 inter-class debating competitions.
Students also participated in the Outstanding Student Citizen Award 2024/25 and the Military
Summer Camp for Hong Kong Youth.


To further strengthen national education and enhance our students’ sense of national identity,
our school adopted a whole school approach, organizing a series of national education
activities. These activities included flag-sharing ceremonies with speeches under the national
flag, talks, visits to the National Security Exhibition Gallery for the whole school, board
displays and exhibitions, paper and online quizzes. There were also sharing sessions and
activities for some designated days specified in the EDB Event Calendar of National
Education. In addition, all students participated in territory-wide and nationwide external
competitions related to national education and national security education, organized by the
Education Bureau and other organisations, to help them become informed and responsible
family members, citizens, nationals, and global citizens.

Academic Performance

Public Examination - HKDSE

respectively. 90.4% of our students met the ‘332A’ requirement or did better in the four core

and Financial Studies (Accounting) (36.4%), Biology (31.4%), Information and
Communication Technology (31.8%), Citizenship and Social Development (100%). The
most outstanding student was He Ruining who achieved 4(5**) and 2(5*), 1(5), A. Apart
from him, seven of our students achieved 5** in at least two subjects.

Rationale for Future Planning: Building a Foundation of Success

Following a comprehensive and collaborative review of school effectiveness—utilizing SSE
tools, stakeholder surveys, programme evaluations, and staff reflections—we have identified
key areas for strategic development. This rationale outlines our planned initiatives, which are
designed to build upon our current successes and further our mission of fostering positive
values and empowering students with new opportunities.


As we embark on the second year of our three-year strategic cycle, our commitment is stronger
than ever. We will actively listen, adapt, and refine our approaches, ensuring that every single
initiative remains powerfully aligned with the evolving needs and aspirations of our students.


1.   Further Developing Positive Education and Student Well-being
     In response to evolving student needs, we are implementing a pioneering Dual Class
     Teacher Programme. This initiative is central to our strategy for promoting student well-
     being and catering to diverse learning styles through dedicated, supportive mentoring
     time.


2.   Providing Targeted Academic Support
     To ensure all students are appropriately challenged and supported, we have expanded our
     academic programmes. These targeted interventions, offered during holidays and after
     school, provide advanced learning for the gifted and foundational support for others,
     empowering every student to achieve their potential.


3.   Strengthening Whole-Person Education
     Career and Life Planning: Our Career and Life Planning programme, bolstered by a
     flourishing Alumni Mentorship and Scholarship scheme, provides critical guidance and
     support, empowering students to make informed decisions about their future paths.


     Language Development: A strategic focus on language building—through targeted
     training and regular practice during assemblies and English activities—has significantly
     boosted student confidence and cultivated professional-level presentation skills.


     Student Support: Our student support framework will continue to integrate discipline with
     counselling, ensuring a holistic and balanced approach to nurturing well-rounded
     individuals.




                                              16
4.   Advancing Digital, STEAM, and AI Education
     As a key strategic priority, we are accelerating our Digital Education, STEAM, and AI
     initiatives. Having laid a strong foundation, we will now integrate advanced technology—
     such as installing smart boards in every classroom—to create a dynamic, future-ready
     learning environment for all students.


5.   Investing in Our Professional Community
     We have launched a new Mentoring Programme and enhanced support for our Middle
     Managers. This investment ensures that all teaching staff, regardless of their career stage,
     receive tailored professional guidance and leadership development opportunities. To
     make professional learning more dynamic and effective, we have planned focused lesson
     observations and job shadowing.


6.   Deepening Values Education
     National Education and Security Education are being systematically strengthened through
     their holistic integration across the curriculum, equipping students with a strong sense of
     civic responsibility and awareness.




Conclusion
Our strategic direction is clear and purposeful. We remain dedicated to refining our initiatives
as needed to provide every student with a fruitful and meaningful school life, firmly centred
on Fostering Positive Values and Empowering Students with New Opportunities.

Evaluation of the Use of the Capacity Enhancement Grant

Strategy 1: To employ one additional native English-speaking tutor to organise various
English activities beyond the class time, establish a new English corner and enrich the English
environment in the whole school, as well as helping teachers to make teaching materials for
the new F.1 Language across the Curriculum and F.4 English Enrichment curriculum, so that
students' language exposure can be maximised and their language proficiency can be
increased.


1.   The English teachers found the workload was reduced because of the additional NET
     helped create teaching materials.
2.   The majority of students enjoyed chatting with the additional NET during the recess and
     lunch time and they enjoyed the activities held in the English Corner.
3.   The English Panel Chairperson and the Head of the Language Enhancement Team shared
     the same opinion that the additional NET successfully enriched the English environment
     of the school and more students were willing to chat with the foreigners.


Strategy 2: To employ one Library Master to help with the daily operations of the library. In
addition, the Library Master will also be responsible for providing resources and support
tailored to meet the needs of students with varying abilities, organizing workshops and
programs that promote literacy and library usage. He/she will also teach students how to use
various technologies and digital tools for research and learning.


1.   The majority of students enjoyed chatting with the additional NET during the recess and
     lunch time and they enjoyed the activities held in the English Corner.
2.   Most teachers agreed that the cross-subject activities and the cultural activities organised
     by the library and various departments benefit the students by widening their exposure.
3.   Teachers’ workload was reduced as the Library Master was enthusiastic about
     cooperating with various departments and organised a wide range of engaging activities.
4.   Teachers agreed that students with varied abilities, ranging from the gifted ones to those
     with learning difficulties, benefit from the reading materials and reading
     programmes/activities organised in the library.
5.   The members of the Reading Club and the student librarians agreed that the library
     operated smoothly throughout the whole school year.
