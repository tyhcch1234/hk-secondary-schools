B.   School Background 創校緣起


     Daughters of Mary Help of Christians Siu Ming Catholic Secondary School is a government-
     subsidized girls' grammar school run by the Daughters of Mary Help of Christians. Mr. Siu
     Ming (1914 - 1986) was an entrepreneur in Hong Kong who generously donated to sponsor
     the funds for the construction of the school. Our school was founded in 1973.


     Daughters of Mary Help of Christians (also known as F.M.A.) is an international Catholic
     congregation of nuns founded by Saint John Bosco and Saint Maria Mazzarello in Mornese,
     Italy in 1872. In 1923, the first Salesian Sisters arrived in China and the Salesian Sisters
     came to Hong Kong in 1950. The F.M.A. has continued to uphold the spirit of the two
     ancestors, and is committed to serving all young people and strive to provide education.

G.   Curriculum of our school 本校課程特點
     In order to cater for the needs of an ever-changing society and the diverse interests and
     abilities of students, our School offers a diversified curriculum e.g. Citizenship, Economics
     and Society / Life and Society in S.1 to S.3, English Literature lessons from an integral part
     of the junior English curriculum and the drama training is incorporated into the S.2 English
     Language curriculum. Students' thinking skills, generic skills, positive values and attitudes
     are developed through the implementation of subject-based or cross-curricular project-based
     learning at all levels, and by the integration of thinking skills into different subjects at the
     junior secondary levels. With regard to elective subjects at senior levels, combinations of
     subjects in the arts, science and business streams are offered to students. Flexibility of
     timetabling enables the school to conduct a wide variety of activities. Other Learning
     Experiences e.g. Aesthetic Education and Career-related Experiences, etc. are offered to
     senior secondary students both within and outside of school hours, which help students
     broaden their horizons.

     本校所提供的課程力求多元化，配合社會需要，並顧及學生的興趣和能力，如中一
     至中三設有公民、經濟與社會科 或生活與社會。初中英文科設有英國文學的課節，
     並將話劇培訓融入中二英文課程中。透過各級進行本科或跨科專題研習，初中於不
     同科目中滲入思考方法，培養學生思維與共通能力、正確價值觀及態度。另外，為
     高中學生選科時提供了文理商兼修的選擇，加上多樣化的上課時間表有利各種活動
     的推行。高中課程更設有美感教育及與工作有關的體驗等校內外「其他學習經歷」
     以擴闊視野。

    Teacher Professional Development: To optimize teachers as Salesian and Professional

Achievements
⚫    The planned development goals were generally achieved, and the implementation
     strategies were carried out effectively
⚫    Staff Development Days, sharing sessions, workshops, and peer lesson observations
     provided useful platforms for professional learning and exchange.
⚫    Teachers were better equipped with the Salesian Family Spirit and were able to apply it in
     their daily teaching and school routines.
⚫    Middle managers demonstrated stronger professional leadership, collaboration, and team
     support, with survey results showing high levels of agreement across the related items.
⚫    The school made substantial efforts to support blended learning, the Rasch Model in
     Mathematics and Science, and induction for new teachers.

⚫    The overall evaluation suggests that the school’s professional development work was
     effective, but there is still room to further strengthen its impact on classroom practice.
⚫    The ambience of professional exchange has been developed, but it can be further enhanced
     through more structured collaboration and deeper peer sharing.
⚫    The effectiveness of staff development activities should continue to be reviewed more
     systematically to ensure they are closely aligned with teachers’ needs and school priorities.
⚫    More focused follow-up is needed to help teachers translate professional learning into
     sustained practice, especially AI in education and values education.
Feedback and Follow-up

    Student Formation and Student Support: To create positive learning experience for our
    students with the ability to live and think positively towards themselves and search for
    meaning in their lives.

Achievements
⚫   Students demonstrated an understanding of the concepts and skills needed to enhance
    positive relationships and positive accomplishment.
⚫   Through the students of the Gifted Education Team, learning experiences were shared with

    proactively volunteered to join the Gifted Education Team, hoping to develop their

    facilitate career planning based on their abilities, and foster a positive and proactive outlook

    the effectiveness of the allocation of the 'Life-wide Learning Grant' within the section,

⚫   Students in general have narrow horizons, they are not willing to participate in activities
    that they are not familiar with.

⚫   The six elements of positive education can be reinforced in future class teacher periods
    whenever necessary.

    progressive manner, utilizing both school-based gifted programs and external gifted
    courses or competitions. By integrating diverse resources, students can develop their gifted
    potential, build a broad knowledge base, and enhance transferable skills, thereby helping
    them to plan the suitable pathways aligned with their gifted abilities.
⚫   The school should provide large varieties of activities and encourage students to join
    activities that they are not familiar with.

    其是在人工智能教育及價值教育方面。

回饋及跟進

⚫   持續舉辦定期教師發展日、工作坊及分享會，以延續教師的專業成長。
⚫   加強協作活動及同儕互相觀摩機會，深化教師之間的專業交流。
⚫   為教師在混合學習、學科教學法及新教學策略的應用方面，提供更具針對性的支援。
⚫   持續並延伸現行措施，支持中層管理人員作為校內的榜樣及專業領袖。

 The feedback and follow-up actions from the previous school year 2024-25:
 (1)   Consider refining professional development activities to further enhance their relevance and usefulness to teachers’ daily work.
 (2) Continue regular evaluation and feedback to sustain high standards of professional leadership.
 (3) Encourage sharing of blended learning best practices and Rasch Model implementation experiences.


2.   Evaluation of the school development plan
     Methods of assessments of school development target –
     Stakeholder Survey (Teacher) on Impression of middle management's professional leadership (questions 24-30) and Teachers' Professional Development
     (questions 31-36) and based on observations during and after the activity.


3.   Review of the school development plan – achievements, reflection, feedback and follow-up
     3.1   Achievements
     1     The planned development goals were generally achieved, and the implementation strategies were carried out effectively
     2     Staff Development Days, sharing sessions, workshops, and peer lesson observations provided useful platforms for professional learning and exchange.
     3     Teachers were better equipped with the Salesian Family Spirit and were able to apply it in their daily teaching and school routines.
     4     Middle managers demonstrated stronger professional leadership, collaboration, and team support, with survey results showing high levels of agreement across
           the related items.
     5     The school made substantial efforts to support blended learning, the Rasch Model in Mathematics and Science, and induction for new teachers.

     3.2 Reflection
     1   The overall evaluation suggests that the school’s professional development work was effective, but there is still room to further strengthen its impact on
         classroom practice.
     2   The ambience of professional exchange has been developed, but it can be further enhanced through more structured collaboration and deeper peer sharing.
     3   The effectiveness of staff development activities should continue to be reviewed more systematically to ensure they are closely aligned with teachers’ needs
         and school priorities.
     4.  More focused follow-up is needed to help teachers translate professional learning into sustained practice, especially AI in education and values education.

     3.3   Feedback and Follow-up
     1     Continue to provide regular Staff Development Days, workshops, and sharing sessions to sustain professional growth.
     2     Strengthen collaborative activities and peer-to-peer learning opportunities to deepen professional exchange among teachers.
     3     Provide targeted support for teachers in blended learning, subject-specific pedagogy, and the application of new teaching strategies.
     4.    Maintain and extend the current practices that support middle managers as role models and professional leaders within the school.

3.1 成就
1    計劃中的發展目標大致已達成，各項推行策略亦順利落實。
2    教師發展日、分享會、工作坊及同儕觀課，為教師提供了良好的專業學習及交流平台。

4.   中層管理人員展現出更強的專業領導、協作及團隊支援能力，相關問卷結果亦顯示同工普遍認同。
5.   學校積極支援混合學習、數學及科學科的 Rasch Model，以及新入職教師的入職培訓。


3.2 反思
1    整體評估顯示，學校的教師專業發展工作成效理想，但仍有空間進一步加強其對課堂實踐的影響。
2    校內專業交流的氛圍已逐步建立，但仍可透過更有系統的協作及更深入的同儕分享加以提升。
3    教師發展活動的成效應持續更有系統地檢視，以確保與教師需要及學校發展重點保持一致。
4.   需要更具針對性的跟進，以協助教師將專業學習轉化為持續而穩定的教學實踐，尤其是在人工智能教育及價值教育方面。

3.1 成就
1    (2A) 全方位學習計劃以及資訊科技創新實驗室的資助,讓學生得以參與 STEAM 工作坊、考察活動及比賽。
2    (2A) 部份參賽的主辦機構更在比賽前提供免費的 STEAM 工作坊,對學生極具吸引力,並能有效幫助他們學習不同的新技能。
3    (2B) 學生對準確表達科學及歷史概念所需的語言技能有更深入的理解。
4    (2B) 融入具吸引力、遊戲化及小組形式的活動，有助提升學生參與度及學習動機。
5    (2B) 中一學生在理解綜合科學課程的語言要求及科學內容方面有明顯進步，問卷整體評分為 6 分滿分中的 4.49 分。
6    (2B) 中二學生在科學寫作中運用適切學術詞彙及銜接手段的能力有所提升，問卷整體評分為 4.32 分（滿分 6 分）
                                                              。
7    (2B) 中二學生在描述及詮釋政治漫畫方面的信心及能力顯著提升（此為歷史資料題重要技能），問卷整體評分為 4.6 分（滿分 6 分）
                                                                      。
8    (2C) 老師認為混合式學習能提升學生的積極性 / 投入程度(55%)，比較上一年度有所增加; 豐富了學生的學習經驗及鼓勵自學(57%)。
9    (2C) 老師透過電子教學工具可加強課堂的互動性和照顧學生學習的多樣性，進一步發展學生的自主學習和協作學習能力，對深化學習有所幫助。
10   (2C) 老師透過推行混合模式的電子學習，結合線上和線下的學習活動以增進學生的學習體驗。
11   (2C) 學與教（L&T）調查顯示，人工智能在不同學科中的採用程度差異顯著。語言類學科表現出尤為深入的應用整合，大量學生主要將人工智能工具用作搜索手
         段和作業輔助。相反，部分選修科和科學科仍報出較高的「未使用」比例。
12   (2C) 從老師觀察，學生在學習中使用人工智能的態度相當積極，接受度很高。
