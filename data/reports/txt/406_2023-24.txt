(1)      Our School
1.1      School Mission

Based on the teaching of the Bible, we are committed to imparting knowledge and
cultivating good behaviours among students. We aspire to instill proper values in students by
educating them about the truth in the Bible and building their belief in God as the Master of
their lives. We endeavour to empower our students to be well-rounded, righteous and
responsible citizens with godly character.


1.2      School Motto

Jesus Christ is the Master of New Life


1.3      School History

Founded in 1969 on Hong Kong Island, the original name of the school was New Life
School. Thanks to the donation of the Lui Ming Choi Foundation, the school was relocated
to its current campus in 1985 and was renamed as NLSI Lui Kwok Pat Fong College.


1.4      School Management

Our school Incorporated Management Committee was founded in 2008.

1.3.2 Recognition of learners of good SDL habits

Target 2:   To enhance students’ learning to learn competence and self-efficacy in learning

Strategies:
2.1     A provision of reading across the curriculum (RaC), STEM learning experiences and
project learning - through the collaboration of subjects in enhancing students’
-             Ability to organize their learning process
-             Ability to learn and act independently
-             Ability to learn and operate within a group
-             Ability to solve problems
-             Ability to discuss (reflect) their learning activities
2.2     To strengthen the use of e-learning to enable learning to take place beyond the
classroom, to engage different types of students, and to enrich their learning experiences

Achievements:
1.      The school aims at developing students’ self-directed learning abilities and catering
to their learning needs. Subjects have applied all or some of the 5 SDL indicators: Goal-
setting, Self-planning, Self-monitoring, Self-evaluation and Revision in designing effective
lessons. We emphasize the importance of pre-lesson preparations and review of objectives.
Effective lesson designs include engaging lead-in activities related students’ pre-lesson
preparation, good use of tiered learning materials, effective use of the school learning
platform and other online learning tools, active peer/group discussions, prompt and positive
peers’/teacher’s feedback, and other self-directed learning strategies. The lessons were
interactive and student-centred. In general, students’ performance was good.
2.      Concerning learning confidence, the results of the stakeholder survey from 2021 to
2024 indicated an upward trend. Both students and parents agreed that students were
confident in learning both inside and outside the classroom. Based on our experience over
the past few years, we have found that the more willing teachers to praise students for their
success, the greater the increase could be seen in their learning confidence.
3.      Students’ problem-solving and hand-on abilities have shown improvement through
various organized activities. Students enjoyed participating in STEAM activities, such as the
STEAM Fair, academic competitions, and project learning, which required cross-curricular
knowledge. Students’ learning experience is enhanced with the renovation of the LKPFC
smart library. The Library Committee collaborated with subject panels in designing activities
to extend students’ learning experience, for instance, immersive learning using VR
technology to simulate real-world scenarios and competitions related to the reading across
the curriculum (RaC).

4.     Subjects continuously updated the LKPFC E-resources platforms to facilitate online
learning. Students develop a habit of SDL, including searching pre-lesson videos, self-
evaluation materials and other learning tools on the platform. The strategy of updating
LKPFC E-resources platform will be incorporated as routine work.

Reflection:
1.      Teachers should encourage students to regulate their learning process by putting
more effort into developing students’ independent learning skills, such as note-taking,
summarizing, recording mistakes and using e-learning platforms effectively.
2.      Teachers expressed their concerns about igniting students’ learning motivation in
L&T Council meetings, panel head meetings and evaluation reports. It is suggested that
igniting students’ motivation could help sustain their learning interest, willingness to
embrace challenges, and maintain a balanced and healthy lifestyle. The school may consider
implementing strategies to create classroom conditions that enhance learning motivation.
3.      The school will continue to provide chances to celebrate students’ successes, which
will optimize their multiple intelligences and raise their learning confidence, thereby
supporting a growth mindset and healthy lifestyle.
4.      To lay a broad and solid knowledge base among students and to increase their
awareness of students’ national and global identities, it is suggested that students are exposed
to problem-based learning related to the global issues outlined in the Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs).
5.      To further increase students’ competency and confidence in using English across
different subjects, curriculum mapping and analysis of common question/answer types could
be conducted. This would help students apply the sentence patterns learnt in English lessons
to other subjects.
6.      To help students cultivate a broader worldview, and appreciate cultural diversity, it is
suggested that more opportunities are created to celebrate students’ success and to share their
learning experiences both locally and overseas.

Feedback and Follow-up:
To enhance the self-directed learning and students’ learning motivation
1.      To further facilitate goal-setting, subjects not only practice goal-setting with students
during lessons, but also try to help students set goals targeting their personal weaknesses.
2.      To facilitate the self-planning and monitoring process, note-taking skills will be
strengthened in junior forms. PSHE KLA will work together to help implement note-taking
strategies to support students’ learning. For senior forms, reflective skills are important to
identify areas for improvement across different subjects. Learning skill training programmes
will be provided for S4 students in the 2024-2025 academic year.
3.      Classroom conditions will be created to stimulate learning motivation by facilitating
the interface between primary and secondary education and implementing different lesson
designs such as using concepts like relevance, competence, relatedness and autonomy in
lessons.

Achievements:
1.       Through events like Christian Assembly and Student Fellowship, students embraced
Christ-like living. Alpha courses engage students in Christian discussions. Religious Week
fosters spiritual growth and values embodiment.
2.       Students developed a growth mindset through Class Motto Competition, overcoming
adversity stories, character-building group games, and prize ceremonies, fostering a sense of
belonging and achievement.
3.       Students navigated life planning through university info days, life planning talks,
career exploration, and guided subject selection, enhancing their self-awareness, interview
skills, and future path clarity.
4.       Student leaders developed essential skills through training and club chairperson
meetings, broadening horizons and enhancing self-confidence.
5.       Students developed a global view through study tours, experiencing diverse cultures,
AI, STEAM, and environmental protection. Cultural activities and roll call sharing further
broadened their horizons, fostering a deeper understanding of global lifestyles and
perspectives.
6.       Students connected with oneself through various activities promoting mental health,
relaxation, and resilience. They developed social skills, emotional health, and self-efficacy
through growth groups, camps, and projects. Moreover, they gained awareness of social
issues and learned to express emotions, relieving stress and building a stronger sense of self.
7.       Students connected with one another through Welcome Assembly and Benediction,
mentorship schemes, and class prayer meetings, fostering a sense of belonging and
community. Inter-house competitions, friendly matches, and sex education workshops
promoted bonding, school spirit, and cyber safety awareness.
8.       Students connected with society through volunteer services, flag-raising ceremonies,
and values education, fostering character development and cultural awareness. Talks on
China's developments and activities for National Constitution Day deepened their
understanding of Chinese culture and achievements. Environmental protection activities
raised awareness, promoting a sense of social responsibility. These activities aligned with the
12 priority values and attitudes in values education.

Reflection:
1.       Student fellowship and Religious Week have nurtured students' spiritual growth.
Through innovative formats and themes, student fellowship will continue to engage students,
featuring speakers with diverse experiences that cater to their interests. Growth mindset
activities are held to enhance unity, recognition, and self-awareness.
2.       Life planning class periods are to be held to introduce students to the concept of
career planning, to foster self-awareness, and to equip students with necessary skills to make
informed decisions about their academic and career pathways.
3.       Students undergo leadership training and are offered increased opportunities to
showcase their leadership abilities.
4.       A range of mental health activities, including Relaxation Days, Failure Experience
Days, mental health courses and growth groups will be held to promote students' mental
wellness.
5.       Activities will be organized to cultivate students' self-discipline, self-confidence,
team spirit, and resilience. Participants will be provided with more opportunities to showcase
their learning achievements and receive rewards. Talks and workshops will be conducted to
raise students' awareness of the importance of proper conduct and prevent them from
violating laws in Hong Kong.

6.     The school will publicly showcase students' experiences, fostering a positive image
and making students feel valued and supported. The Student Union will serve as a liaison
between students and school administrators, promoting regular communication.
7.     National education activities will be held to foster a sense of national identity among
students.

Feedback and Follow-up:
1.       To nurture spiritual growth and a growth mindset in students, a range of gospel
activities, including Bible classes, student fellowships, and class meetings, will be organized
to cultivate a positive attitude towards life.
2.       Students will explore diverse career paths through interactions with professionals,
workplace visits, internships, and job shadowing. S3 students will attend University Info
Day to understand university requirements for appropriate subject selection in S4.
3.       Themed overseas study tours will be organized to broaden students' perspectives.
Through the Sister School Scheme, our school establishes a connection with a mainland
school.
4.       The school publicly showcases students' experiences, creating positive images to
foster students' sense of value and support. Certain activities, such as Dress Casual Day, will
be rescheduled to the first term. The Student Union intensifies its pivotal role by organizing
regular meetings, aiming to cultivate transparent communication and fruitful partnerships
between students and school administrators.
5.       Values education, national education, sex education, mental health education, and
environmental education hold significant importance for students. Efforts will be made to
raise students' awareness and concern for Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) and
Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) issues.
6.       The 'Speech under the National Flag' will be delivered by various committees and
subject panels.
7.       Stretching exercises will be incorporated into roll call to help students relax and
boost moderate exercise frequency. The Student Union and Houses will organize various
competitions to promote a healthy lifestyle among students.
