SCHOOL-BASED MOI POLICY
In the school year 2025-26, our school will continue to adopt English as the medium of instruction for all forms, except for the following subjects: Chinese
Language, Chinese Literature, Chinese History, Physical Education (F.3-6), Putonghua, Religious & Moral Education and Visual Arts (Senior Secondary).

Rationales

1. To provide our students with more opportunities to be exposed to, and use English at all levels.

2. To enhance our students’ ability to learn through English and to better prepare them for further studies and work in the future.
3. The school has fulfilled the three prescribed criteria set out in the Report on Review of Medium of Instruction by the Education Commission in 2005:
    a. Students possess the ability to learn through English;
    b. Teachers possess the capability to teach through English; and
    c. The school has adequate support strategies / measures.

ENGLISH LANGUAGE SUPPORT MEASURES
                 Objectives                                                                                   Strategies
1. Sustain MOI policy                                 English is used during class time for EMI subjects at all times, including lesson delivery, questioning, responses,
                                                       discussions, handouts, textbooks, test and exam papers, class work and homework.
2. Encourage participation in English speaking        All students are encouraged to join the Hong Kong Schools Speech Festival in the categories of solo verse, prose
   activities                                          reading, public speaking and dramatic duologues.
                                                      English debates are held to provide opportunities for students to strengthen their speaking skills.
                                                      Students are invited to give speeches as public speaking is a good platform for them to polish English language skills.
                                                      S1 students participate choral speaking.
                                                      S2 students participate in English green fashion show.
                                                      S3 students participate in booth-design competitions or debates.
3. Cultivate interest in books & newspapers in        All S1 to S6 students are encouraged to read English newspapers online.
   English                                            All S1 to S3 students subscribe online reading platforms as a RAC supplement.
                                                      Students take turns to present book reviews in reading sessions on Tuesdays.
                                                      An English book fair is organized every year.
                                                      A read log to train students’ reading skills is issued.
                                                      A recommended booklist is provided for all students.
4. Promote the use of English on campus               The English Department promotes and runs English activities regularly.
                                                      English Club members take the initiative to organize English-speaking activities in the English Learning Centre.
                                                      Publication Team publishes English publication.
5. Create an English-rich environment                 Morning assemblies in school are conducted in English.
                                                      MC Teams members will be invited to host different school events, such as Investiture of Student Leaders, Speech
                                                       Day and Prize Presentation in English.
                                                      English language teachers speak English with students beyond class time.
                                                      Bulletin boards are decorated in English.
                                                      Announcements are made in English.
                                                      English activities are held throughout the year.
6. Support self-learning in English                   All students are encouraged to use online self-directed learning resources and required to do book sharing and news
                                                       sharing in class.
7. Cultivate interest in English learning             The fun aspects of learning English through movie appreciation, popular culture, fashion, etc. are emphasized.
8. Cater for learner diversity in learning English    Extra work and special schemes are tailored for S1 elite and weaker classes.
