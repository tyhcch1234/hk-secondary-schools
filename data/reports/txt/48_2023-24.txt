(1) Vision and Mission
Tak Oi Secondary School is sponsored by the Missionary Sisters of the Immaculate Conception
(M.I.C.). Through a whole school approach, we aim to provide quality Catholic Education to lead
our young girls into a life of joy and thanksgiving.


Love and Truth, our school motto, are the guiding principles for developing our girls and the
whole school. Amazed at God’s gracious love, we nurture in our girls a heart of appreciating all
that is good, true and beautiful. Acknowledging Jesus as the ultimate Truth, we enlighten our
girls to strive for excellence in their pursuit of knowledge and righteousness in life.


We are committed to fostering a caring, respectful and joyful environment with a rich array of
learning opportunities for our young girls to develop their unique potentials in all aspects and
to become a whole person.


We embrace the call to educate our girls to become responsible, resilient, creative, passionate
and collaborative young women who dedicate themselves to self-growth and to contributing to
the common good of the universe.

(2) Achievements and Reflections on Major Concerns
The academic year 2023 – 2024 was the last year of the 3-year development cycle (2021/22-
2023/24) with the theme “Promoting Holistic Wellness for the Common Good of All”, aiming to
empower students to become self-reflective learners through strengthening their learning
competence as well as to foster students’ positive aspiration for whole-person development
through deepening PERMA education.

Major Concern 1: To empower students to become self-reflective learners through
strengthening their learning competence

Strengthening students’ learning competence was the prime objective of school development.
In the 3-year development cycle, the school would mainly focus on increasing students’
confidence and effectiveness in learning by enriching their learning experiences, enhancing
their learning skills, implementing assessment as and for learning, as well as fostering ongoing
teacher professional development.

Priority Task 1: Enriching students’ learning experiences

Students were expected to learn confidently by building on their strengths and unleashing their
potentials through school-based curriculum review, increase in meaningful learning exposure
and an enhancement of interdisciplinary learning.

 Achievements

 The school curriculum was further developed to ensure a broad and balanced curriculum to
 be offered to junior form students through introduction of Citizenship, Economics and
 Society, History and Geography in the PSHE Key Learning Area. This could help the school to
 nurture students’ social competence, attend to students’ personal growth more
 comprehensively and emphasize history and culture for deepening moral and civic values.

 From the Stakeholders’ Survey of Teachers in 2023 – 2024, most teachers continued to agree
 that the school curriculum aligned with the education development trends and was broad
 and balanced enough to facilitate students’ whole-person development.

 Reflection

 The LaC policy in the school was found effective and advanced in which subject departments
 developed sufficient awareness and readiness to help students learn with English as the
 medium of instruction. The collaboration between different subjects and the English
 Language Department was also professional and beneficial with continuous progress and
 new elements that could enable students to arouse their interests in the subjects and English
 as well as to learn the subject knowledge and skills more effectively.

 IT in education and e-learning tools had become an integral part of students’ learning and
 could be beneficial in managing knowledge and developing self-directed learning. The school
 would strengthen the education of information literacy so as to equip students with
 necessary knowledge, skills and attitude on proper use of e-learning tools and information
 online. Such an education had been integrated in various learning experiences of students in
 school but it would be more effective if teachers could further highlight its importance and
 provide concrete directions or solutions to students during their teaching or students’
 completion of learning tasks.

 Feedback and follow-up

 Reviewing students’ situation of the use of information and technology tools, the school
 would focus on using the electronic device and tools on students’ engagement and
 participation in lessons activities as well as instant feedback in S1 – S3 lessons. They would
 form a better habit of using electronic tools to learn and develop more effective learning
 routines. Information literacy elements would also be embedded in learning and teaching to
 raise students’ awareness on the proper and wise use of information technology. The school
 would also make good use of the Enhanced My Pledge to Act for increasing students’
 information and media literacy through various learning experiences and exposure. To
 further enhance the effectiveness of using e-learning tools in different subjects, subject
 departments would propose their preferred education apps that could better enrich
 students’ learning.

 Riding on students’ high willingness and motivation to improve themselves, the school would
 strengthen students’ capability on high order thinking skills and self-management skills,
 particularly on creative problem-solving skills, task prioritisation and expectation
 management. With teachers’ guidance and opportunities to question and express, students
 would be able to generate more solutions and creative suggestions to tackle different
 learning situations. This would not only help students flexibly manage different challenges in
 their learning or growth, but also encourage students to transfer their acquired knowledge
 and skills in tackling various situations.

Major Concern 2: To foster students’ positive aspiration for whole-person development
through deepening PERMA Education

Nurturing our students to become a whole person as well as cultivating positive values have
been the vision and mission of our school. Through creating and strengthening positive emotion,
engagement, relationship, meaning and achievements, our students will have a heightened level
of confidence, self-awareness and flourishing wellbeing that enable them to make informed
choices to live a more fulfilling life that is aligned with their values and interests.

Priority Task 1:    Fostering self-management, collaboration, and creativity while
empowering initiative and resilience in students.

The school has implemented measures to boost students' self-management, collaboration, and
creativity. Through diverse activities and initiatives, students are encouraged to foster initiative
and resilience.

 Achievements

 The school provided a wide range of programs and activities aiming at helping students
 understand themselves, display their talents, and foster positive values. These included class-
 based masses, assemblies, religious sharing sessions, and recommended values for students
 in S1 to S6. Students participated in activities that encouraged appreciation for others,
 celebrated diversity, and equipped them with the knowledge and skills needed to establish
 meaningful career and life goals. Through activities and counselling, they gained insights into
 their interests, strengths, and values, empowering them to make informed choices and
 pursue paths that align with their aspirations.

 Under the theme "Let Our Light Shine," a variety of programs were organized, including
 sharing sessions by MIC sisters who discussed real-life experiences to help students
 recognize the importance of showcasing their talents.

 To promote the values of respect and resilience, the school introduced a series of sharing
 sessions and interactive activities. These sessions covered topics like active listening and the
 importance of respecting family members, fostering open dialogue and encouraging students
 to reflect on their relationships and communication skills.


Tak Oi Secondary School   School Report 2023-2024   Page 11
 Additionally, the school held a 10-Day Challenge that encouraged students to take concrete
 actions in their daily life for reinforcing these values. Together, these initiatives aimed to
 create an environment that motivated students to embrace respect and resilience in their
 daily lives, enhancing their overall personal growth.

 The Career and Life Planning Team organized various workshops to illuminate diverse career
 paths for students. Notably, the coffee latte art workshop received positive feedback, with
 participants earning certificates to recognize their skills. The Handicraft Workshop also
 highlighted exceptional artistic talent among S1 students.

 The Floristry Workshop deepened students' understanding of the floral industry, fostering
 creativity and career aspirations. Participants created a backdrop for the S6 Graduation
 ceremony and crafted floristry items for charity on Mission Day, showcasing their talents
 while contributing to the community.

 The S4 Experiential Leadership Programme enhances leadership skills in proactive,
 resilience, self-management, and creativity among S4 students through various activities
 such as class exercises, reading, and self-reflection. Teachers noted that students actively
 engaged in the program, reflecting on their strengths and weaknesses, sharing thoughts with
 peers, and fostering a culture of respect. The class-based activities also improved intra-class
 communication and strengthened class spirit, reinforcing key values

 To promote a healthy lifestyle, a "Healthy Living Week" was launched in the 2023-24,
 highlighting the vital importance of health for overall well-being. The initiative encouraged
 healthy habits through engaging activities, cooking classes for nutritious meals, and
 opportunities to try new sports. A whole-school morning exercise event fostered community
 and reinforced the significance of regular physical activity. Inter-house basketball
 competitions promoted teamwork, while singing bowl workshops and breathing exercises
 enhanced mindfulness. This proactive approach empowered students to make positive
 choices that supported their physical and mental well-being, emphasizing that a healthy
 lifestyle is essential for their success and happiness.

 Reflection

 Overall, these initiatives empowered students to embrace a healthy lifestyle and make
 positive choices, establishing a solid foundation for their future success and happiness. The
 thoughtful integration of programs supported both personal and academic growth,
 preparing students to face future challenges while fostering respect, resilience, and
 creativity.

 The sharing session offers a valuable opportunity for students to find inspiration and engage
 in meaningful reflection. During these sessions, students were encouraged to listen to diverse
 perspectives and experiences, which can motivate them to think critically about their own
 journeys. This environment not only fostered personal growth but also allowed students to
 connect with one another on a deeper level, enhancing their understanding of themselves
 and the world around them. Through thoughtful dialogue and shared experiences, students
 gained insights that inspire positive change in their lives.

 The Value Education Team created the 10-Day Challenge to encourage students to reflect on
 real-life experiences, highlighting the significance of hands-on engagement and meaningful

Tak Oi Secondary School   School Report 2023-2024   Page 12
 self-reflection. This initiative aimed to foster personal growth and a deeper understanding of
 the challenges they may face in everyday life. However, it was observed that some students
 did not fully commit to completing the tasks. Instead, they gravitated toward easier options
 that allowed them to avoid genuine challenges and personal growth opportunities. By
 choosing less demanding activities, they missed the chance to develop resilience and confront
 obstacles that would ultimately enhance their learning experience.

 The S4 Experiential Leadership Programme enhanced students' leadership skills in
 proactive, resilience, self-management, and creativity. Through class exercises and self-
 reflection, students engaged with their strengths and weaknesses, fostered respect and
 improved communication. However, the program could benefit from a stronger focus on
 solving real-life problems and connecting with the community, which would further enrich
 the learning experience and better prepare students for future challenges.

 Healthy Living Week effectively promoted health and well-being but the limited time for
 activities could not make an immense impact on the lifestyle of students. The stakeholder
 survey revealed that only 25% of students felt they could maintain a healthy lifestyle through
 a regular routine, moderate exercise, and stress management. This low figure suggested a
 need for increased awareness and resources related to healthy living.

 Feedback and Follow-up

 Sharing sessions featuring teachers, students, and alumni will continue to provide real-life
 examples that inspire students and serve as role models for character development. By
 engaging with diverse perspectives, students will gain valuable insights into personal growth
 and resilience, fostering a supportive community that encourages reflection on their own
 values and aspirations.

 To strengthen the focus on solving real-life problems and connecting with the community in
 the S4 Experiential Leadership Programme, service-learning projects can be integrated that
 require students to identify and address local issues. This hands-on approach enables them
 to apply their leadership skills while making a positive impact.

 The Value Education Team will launch a revised 5-Day Challenge that focuses on specific real-
 world contexts that present authentic challenges. By engaging in targeted activities, students
 will develop resilience and problem-solving skills, better preparing them for the realities of
 life.

 To develop healthy habits requires a long-term commitment, not just a single week.
 Therefore, for future events, extending the time for each activity and integrating these
 themes throughout the academic year will significantly enhance focus and engagement. This
 approach will reinforce the importance of a healthy lifestyle and ensure that everyone can
 fully maximize the benefits of the experience.

                                            Report on the Use of the Promotion of Reading Grant
                                                          2023-2024 School Year

 Part 1: Evaluation of the Effectiveness

 1. Evaluation of the objective: (e.g. evaluating if the objective of “creating a reading culture” is achieved and any change is required based on
    student attitude, loan of books, engagement in reading activities)

      a. Students showed enthusiasm and high engagement in various library activities, including thematic book displays, reading activities,
         author talks, the “Read-Think-Talk” program, and reading clubs. Students’ active participation and positive attitude are greatly
         appreciated.
      b. The checkout rate increased satisfactorily, with students expressing interest in new books, classic books, and teacher-recommended
         books.


 2. Evaluation of strategies: (e.g. evaluating the effectiveness of strategies employed to create a reading culture based on the change of number of
    books borrowed by students, the scope of the reading activities)

      a. Nine thematic book displays, and three related reading activities took place, followed by games. More than 300 students participated in
         each game, the participant numbers were satisfactory, and these events successfully motivated students to read.
      b. Six book recommendations were presented during the morning assembly, where students were attentive and eager to borrow the
         recommended books. The six recommended books were borrowed nearly 40 times throughout the year.
      c. Two author talks were held for S1-S3 students. Our team invited authors 譚劍 and 金鈴 as speakers. The topics of the authors talks are
         “Detective stories” and “Travel literature” respectively. The topics engaged students and received high ratings in activity surveys.
         Students rated “Detective stories” 3.89 out of 5 and “Travel literature” 3.95 out of 5.
      d. Our team organized two reading clubs this year, featuring Ms. Natasha Tsang (the principal) and Ms. Lydia Ouyang (the Chinese teacher).
         They explored “The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse” and “Counselling for Toads: A Psychological Adventure,” respectively. Each
         session included in-depth discussions and related reading recommendations, resulting in positive feedback from students. Students rated
         the reading clubs 4.2 out of 5.
