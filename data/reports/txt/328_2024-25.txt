1. Our School


School Background
Shun Lee Catholic Secondary School is an aided co-educational secondary school founded in 1982 by the
Hong Kong Catholic Diocese, which has a mission for her schools to promote the formation of the whole
person. The school motto SIC DEO PLACET “悅樂主心” reflects our commitment to realize this
mission of the sponsoring body.


School Motto
SIC DEO PLACET
The Biblical Origin:
“So whether we are at home or away, we make it our aim to please Him” (2 Corinthians 5:9)
Message: May we live according to the will of God


School Mission
The School is dedicated to providing a positive learning environment in which students understand the
truth of the Gospel and experience the love of Christ. It is where students learn to integrate Christian faith
with Chinese culture and life for holistic personal development.
We believe that students are unique individuals possessing distinctive gifts and talents. We thus commit
ourselves to helping students develop their potential to the fullest and become independent learners
capable of mastering self-learning skills, with emphasis on fostering positive attitudes towards life.
We devote ourselves to educating students to become responsible citizens who are eager and competent
to make contributions to society, and to encourage them to carry on the best of Chinese culture.


Core Values of School
As a Catholic school, we shall uphold and pass on the core values of TRUTH, JUSTICE, LOVE, LIFE
and FAMILY to our students to prepare them properly for their life and future responsibility. The
school also works in accordance with the school mission and dedicates itself to cultivating the core values
of “Love”, “Respect”, “Responsibility”, “Perseverance”, “Thanksgiving”, and “In Pursuit of Excellence”
in students.

2.1 Major Concern 1: Optimizing Self-Regulated Learning to Nurture Students into
    Proactive Learners
 Achievements

  In a bid to develop proactive learners, which is essential in today’s rapidly evolving society,
  we place a strong emphasis on fostering self-regulated learning among students. To achieve
  this goal, our teachers have enhanced our school-based academic framework of the 4Ps (i.e.
  preparation, participation, presentation and possession) by integrating e-Learning strategies.
  Besides, our teachers have designed subject-based and/or cross-curricular mini research
  projects to ignite students’ curiosity and further stretch their learning potential. We have also
  strengthened assessment literacy, encouraging teachers to utilize assessment data to identify
  students’ learning difficulties, provide constructive feedback, and refine both curriculum
  planning and implementation to improve learning outcomes.
  Target 1: Enhancing Students’ Self-Regulated Learning Skills
  Over the past academic year, subject teachers implemented a variety of e-Learning strategies
  (e.g. e-Learning tools, blended learning, and artificial intelligence) in the 4Ps. These
  strategies have effectively enhanced students’ self-regulated learning skills both during
  lessons and beyond. Furthermore, teachers have provided an increased number of self-access
  digital learning materials on Google Classroom and Google Sites, as well as designed diverse
  subject-based and/or cross-curricular mini research projects to foster students’ self-learning
  skills and bolster their self-regulated learning.
  The results of this year’s school-based learning and teaching survey indicated that students
  have responded positively to their learning in the 4Ps, the e-Learning strategies employed
  by the teachers, and the overall effectiveness of teaching and learning.

Target 2: Stretching Students’ Learning Capacity
(i) Student Learning Profile (SLP)


With the aim of stretching students' learning capacity, we have placed a greater emphasis
on Student Learning Profile (SLP). Through SLP, we hope to empower students to take
ownership of their learning by setting clear goals and actively monitoring their progresses.
These skills are critical for cultivating proactive learners.


Quantitatively, all students participated in the school-based SLP scheme and submitted their
learning reflections in 2024/2025. Qualitatively, it has been observed that students have
become more conscientious in reflecting on their learning, as evidenced by the increased
detail and depth in their reflective journals compared to previous years. Student’s reflections
were more concrete and realistic compared to previous years. The positive results are further
supported by the satisfactory findings in SHS and APASO surveys. Details are as follows:

(ii) Reading across the Curriculum (RaC)


One to two RaC lessons are scheduled within each cycle of the school timetable. An annual
plan was devised to outline the specific texts to be read throughout the year. While Chinese
and English language teachers take the leading role in teaching RaC, subject heads play a
crucial role in disseminating the value of reading with their panel members, ensuring that
all subject teachers contribute to the selection of the reading materials tailored to various
student levels. Last year, we expanded our resource library significantly, adding 35 new sets
of reading materials. These materials encompass all subjects, and are aligned to the specific

the student survey are satisfactory. More than 75% of the students reported that they read
the materials using specific reading strategies, which reflects the effectiveness of the RaC
lessons.

(iii) Cross-/Co-Curricular Activity


In regard to KPM21 (參與全港性校際比賽的學生百分比), which measures the percentage
of students participating in territory-wide interschool competitions, a diverse array of 114
events across various domains have been recorded, including activities in STREAM (Science,
Technology, Reading, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics), Languages, Physical and
Aesthetic Education, National Education etc.


According to the KLA records, our Life-wide Learning (LWL) activities encompass a total
of 272 Other Learning Experience (OLE) events within and outside the school, amounting to
79857 hours of student engagement in activities, an average of over 105 hours of participation
per student.


In 2024/2025 academic year, our students have shown a significant commitment to social
services, with 59% of the entire student population engaging in more than 10 hours of services
and 39% of students dedicating 20 hours or more. Each student participated in seven or more
events, contributing to the total of 79,857 hours amassed by our 764 students.


(iv) Gifted Education and Talent Pool


The Gifted Education Team has effectively utilized a selection tool to identify students for
participation in a variety of activities both within and outside the school. This talent pool was
also presented to subject teachers during staff meetings, fostering broader awareness and
engagement among all teachers.
Teachers have reported that the talent pool is easily accessible and highly beneficial when
nominating students for programs. According to the Gifted Education Team, the number of

    Reflection

     This year marks the launch of our refined three-year school development plan. Building on
     the proven success of the 4Ps teaching pedagogy in nurturing proactive and self-regulated
     learners, the new annual school plan has integrated a wider range of e-Learning strategies,
     including artificial intelligence, to further enhance teaching and learning effectiveness. The
     elaborated Student Learning Profile (SLP) has significantly boosted students’ motivation,
     engagement, self-reflection, and character strength.
     Additionally, the enhanced gifted education workflow, increased co- and cross-curricular
     activities, and refined Reading across the Curriculum (RaC) materials have complemented
     our holistic approach to self-regulated learning. The success of these initiatives is evidenced
     by our school-based surveys, the Stakeholders’ Survey, and the APASO-III survey.

    Feedback and Follow-up



     Optimizing self-regulated learning to nurture students into proactive learners in both subject-
     based and activity-based learning will remain a top priority. To enhance students’ learning
     effectiveness across various subjects, teachers will continue to integrate e-Learning
     strategies into the school-based 4Ps academic framework and leverage artificial intelligence
     in both teaching and learning processes. All subjects will focus on utilizing assessment data
     more effectively to identify learning difficulties, provide targeted feedback, and refine
     curricula to improve teaching pedagogies and students’ learning outcomes.
     In addition to the optimized 4Ps pedagogy, the school will foster proactive, self-regulated
     learners through co- and cross-curricular activities. These initiatives will be complemented
     by the SLP to enhance self-reflection, RaC to broaden their knowledge base, and gifted
     education programs to help students reach their full potential.

Reflection

The school has laid a solid foundation in promoting the value education of "Thanksgiving," with students
generally showing positive understanding and willingness to express gratitude. However, the data also
reveal challenges in translating this understanding and willingness into sustained behavioral practice and
deeper spiritual internalization. Particularly, regarding the spiritual dimension of "glorifying God's grace,"
students show a relatively lower level of acceptance.


In the future, experiential learning and the promotion of daily gratitude practices can be strengthened. Value
education is an ongoing journey that requires continuous reflection, adaptation, and commitment from the
entire school community. Through persistent effort, the school can nurture students who not only understand
gratitude but also integrate it into their lives, becoming truly grateful people.

It is difficult to encourage students to keep writing “Thanksgiving journal” regularly, and therefore it is
suggested to be changed to be a one-off campaign.

is noted that the score for Q1 in APASO-III (Physical Health), which measures students’ awareness of
healthy lifestyles, stands at 2.3. This presents an opportunity for further growth and development in
promoting awareness of healthy living among students.

From the observation of teachers, the students were willing to do the morning exercise. The coaches of the
sports teams reported that the members got involved seriously in the training activities. However, during
the focus group interview, students reflected that they rarely did exercise after school. Despite the
introduction of MVPa60 mobile applications, specifically 'Robocoach' to encourage students to exercise
more at home, these applications were not user-friendly enough.

The post-event survey indicates that the Assembly talks were highly effective. Feedback from teachers'
observations, focus group interviews, and surveys reveals that a significant number of students are
experiencing stress. Overall, mass and group activities, as well as workshops, were successful. However,

Feedback and Follow-up

To emphasize a healthy lifestyle, the school will continue to strengthen the promotion of healthy living next
year. We hope that students can achieve holistic well-being physically, mentally, and socially.


It is recommended to continue meditation sessions in the upcoming year. Additionally, it is suggested that
Religious Education lessons be utilized to provide students with a deeper understanding of the significance
of meditation and to guide them in experiencing it during these lessons.

During the morning assembly, a weekly morning exercise session will be introduced, requiring all students
to participate in stretching exercise for approximately 3 to 5 minutes in the court or hall. Additionally,
Physical Education teachers will actively encourage students to utilize the MVPa60 apps for further exercise
beyond school hours. An award and incentive system will be established to promote consistent use of these
applications.

The existing strategies, such as providing guidance on proper manners before events—including workshops,
career talks, sharing sessions, and activities outside school—will continue as routine practice.

However, some initiatives like the "Tree Hole" emotional support space had limited uptake and will be
reviewed.

Assembly talks on mental health and stress management received high positive feedback. In future,
assembly talk themes will focus more on stress management, and anti-bullying and leadership programs
will continue supporting a harmonious school climate.

      3. 學校牧民工作方向及目標

           3.1 學校牧民工作方向及目標

               > 加深學生對天主教信仰的認識

               > 深化心靈成長

               > 推動天主教五大核心價值

               > 讓同學正向成長

           3.2 計劃對學校牧民工作發展的幫助

               > 提昇祈禱及宗教氣氛

               > 深化同學認識信仰

           3.3 推行學校牧民工作所遇到的困難

               > 學生因為學業或課外活動繁忙，需多加鼓勵同學參加宗教活動

               > 缺乏足夠活動空間或機會，與同學接觸及分享信仰

      4. 牧民助理協助以下的活動

4.1 全校性宗教活動
內容:           開學禮儀、 聖誕禮儀、四旬期饑饉午餐及靈修書展、希望的朝聖者、下午課前默想
對象及人數 :       全校師生
簡述活動內容及成效 :
開學彌撒 : 以「感恩」為主題， 由蔡俊源神父主祭，並由學校合唱團帶領唱聖詠、同學負責不同的禮儀崗位, 讓
全校師生能參與並感受彌撒禮儀的氣氛， 透過神父講道反思感恩。整體師生反應正面，專注禮儀訊息。
聖誕禮儀 : 聖誕聯歡當天在禮堂進行表演及遊戲，同學於喜樂的氣氛下，明白聖誕的意義。
四旬期饑饉午餐 : 社會服務機構於禮堂舉行講座，讓同學認識其服務工作，同學自願於午餐時進食麵包，將購買
的金錢用作捐助該機構的服務對象。
四旬期靈修書展 : 讓同學增加對信仰及教會的認識。
希望的朝聖者 : 配合禧年，訂購 luce 讓同學領養，傳揚希望，為同學建立正向和積極人生。
下午課前默想 : 由校長帶領老師及同學，或以歌曲/音樂，進行默想。


4.2 祈禱氣氛
內容 :          早禱、懷念教宗及為他祈禱展板
對象及人數 :       全校師生
簡述活動內容及成效:
早禱 : 讓同學在祈禱的氛圍下成長，習慣每日之始，由祈禱開始。
懷念前任教宗及為他祈禱展板 : 讓同學認識教宗及其生平，自發為他祈禱

對象及人數 :    全校師生

定期更換展板主題， 以提升同學對教會的認識。另外，張貼於壁報板及宗教室外的海報亦讓同學知悉的最近天主

4.4 信仰培育
內容 :       馬來西亞朝聖之旅、本地朝聖之旅、基督小先鋒領袖培訓、學生福音營、公教老師午餐聚會、學
           生信仰小團隊、堅振班、中三 Alpha、5D 靈修營、天主教同學會
對象及人數:     個別班級、老師、學生
簡述活動內容及成效:
舉辦不同類型的活動，讓老師及同學進一步認識教會，檢視自己內心，加深信仰，並在信仰的氛圍下培養自己與
群體之間的友誼。
Alpha 啟發青年影片播放及分享活動在午膳期間進行, 邀請了中三級感興趣的同學以及天主教同學會幹事協辦。同
學共進午膳, 並一同觀看影片加深信仰認識, 並進行討論分享自己經驗、看法和感受。同學反映觀看影片后釐清對
信仰的誤解, 加深與身邊人的關係。然而部分同學認為單純觀看影片的活動沉悶， 而影片會單向灌輸對信仰的看
法。
