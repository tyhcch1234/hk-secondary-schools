                                                        Our School
      Immaculate Heart of Mary is a government aided co-educational secondary school founded by the Franciscan
Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows in 1981. The construction of the school campus was completed on 27th
August, 1981 and the official opening and blessing ceremony was held on 9th February, 1983 by The Most Rev.
Bishop John B. Wu, Rev. Sr. M. Francis OSF & Mr. Chan Yan Pang SEO. The school came into existence with 17
teachers and 6 classes of Form 1 and Form 4 respectively. With the support and guidance from the Franciscan
sisters, the school has optimized its class structure in face of changing needs of the society. The current student
population is totaled 766 with 24 classes in F.1 to F6.

                                        A Vision of a Catholic Religious School
       As a staff member of a Catholic school, we should carry out the following core values of Hong Kong Catholic
Education: Truth, Justice, Love, Life and family. We should also follow the Mission and Vision of the School
Sponsoring Body – Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows, and the Guidelines for Franciscan
Education – Go and Teach.
       At the beginning of educational practice, we as Franciscan, share the Church’s commitment to building the
world. We are aware that this social promotion, carried out with the Gospel spirit, is the expression and the sign of
the presence of the Kingdom of God in our society, a work that helps us deepen the educational and anthropological
vision developed by Franciscan Masters.
       In Franciscan spirituality, the concepts of brotherhood and minority constitute the centre or the fundamental
nucleus of interpersonal relationships.     The fraternity is the vital and proper place of education. It is the place where
various values meet and are shared. The values of acceptance, dialogue, respect for diversity, equality, responsibility,
familiarity, trust, happiness, optimism, peace and forgiveness.
       Minority quantifies and directs relations with others. This value, being the basis for all relations, makes us
simple and obliging, and helps us to move closer to the other with an open heart and ready to discover his inner self.
A style or way of relating which is opposed to any intention of ownership and domination of persons or property and
which prevents any inferiority complex or contempt of itself.         In the context of fraternity and minority it is important
to educate on poverty, solidarity, service to each other, serious work and building peace.

                                                School Mission Statement
       Immaculate Heart of Mary College is sponsored by the Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows.
Through the example of BLESSED VIRGIN MARY in the LIFE of JESUS, we are led to imitate HER
COMPASSIONATE LOVE and WILLING WORKS of SERVICE TO OTHERS. Empowered by the spirit of
Gospel, we are dedicated to providing a caring and supportive school environment so that learning is valued,
mutual respect is shared and all members can develop their potential to the fullest capacity. By means of our
concern for Catholic Faith, Personal Formation and Scholarly Development, we strive to prepare students to
become responsible citizens who contribute to society, are sensitive to the needs of others and meet the challenges
of today’s world.

                                                       Catholic Faith
      We provide an atmosphere whereby each student is encouraged to seek a relationship with God through generous personal
response to the Gospel and by acquiring an active understanding and experience of the Catholic tradition.

Religious Education and Liturgical Activities
      Since we are a Catholic institute of education, Christian values are to be upheld and exemplified through religious
education. Moreover, students are encouraged to participate daily in prayer, to join the school community in various
liturgical activities, availing themselves of these opportunities to reflect their faith and lifestyle.

       We seek to provide all members of the college, working harmoniously under a common community spirit,
the experience of a genuine sense of belonging, where each one finds charity, support, gentleness, kindness and
tolerance.

                                                  Personal Formation
       We aim to develop each student’s value, self-esteem and career through personal guidance in which the
students realize their self-identity, are recognized as individuals, and experience a caring, supportive school
environment.

Holistic Development Approach
       Students are encouraged to reach for excellence in every dimension of their lives (spiritual, moral, cognitive,
emotional, physical, aesthetic and social) through the range of activities offered in the school so that they will grow
towards maturity as persons of character and education.
       Such approach seeks to reveal and develop each student’s character, abilities and leadership potential through
our concern for their religious, emotional, physical, academic and psychological needs.

Career Preparation
       By means of a balanced curriculum, we prepare our students for their own free, informed, conscientious
choices in study, work and life.      The essence of our career guidance programs is to assist students to qualify
themselves for further education and careers, and to pursue growth as an individual, capable of independence and
responsible life decisions.

                                                Scholarly Development
       We seek to maximize the achievement opportunities of our students so that they can acquire self-motivation
and independent thinking, respect for knowledge and the search for truth.

Persevering Attitudes
       Students are encouraged to develop their intellectual skills through consistent hard work, regular study habits
and a fulfilling of their duties.

                                                   General Outcomes
       So that each student will live Catholic values, serve humanity and society, and grow towards maturity as a
person of character, of education and of charity, our college aims to develop:
1.     a college community that is genuinely interested in and supportive of all members, fostering individual growth
       and development.
2.     Catholic values, respect for the values of others, and the ability to critically evaluate the current social issues.
3.     an all-round development in moral, spiritual, cognitive, emotional, physical, social and aesthetic education.
4.     an awareness and challenge for students towards their responsibilities to love the less fortunate and the less
       privileged, who hold priority in our care and concern.
5.     the individual interests and abilities of students, for the betterment of their personal welfare and that of society.

                                                   Staff Commitment
       Our staff is committed to the total formation of our students through our dedication to the ideals of Catholic
education and of our college, acting as examples for the students and upholding a life-long learning attitude aimed
towards providing quality education to nurture younger generations.

                                                           Our Vision
       Being a professional educational organization which has inherited the education values of the Hong Kong
Province Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows, we adhere to our school mission to practice the
spirit of the Gospel – loving God and people.
       We envisage that all teaching and administrative staff in the Immaculate Heart of Mary family are dedicated
professionals who keep abreast of the trends in teaching. They are not only role models of students, but also caring
and committed teachers.
       We hope our students will develop simplicity and wholesomeness, while becoming intellectually competent
to learn actively and think independently. Being nurtured to be young leaders who can shoulder responsibility, they
also imitate St. Francis to be a channel of peace in communities.
       We envision that through parent-teacher collaboration and effective utilization of community resources,
students will develop their potential to full capacity. With a sense of social awareness and responsibility, they will be
compassionate in serving others and contributing to communities.

                                                         School Facilities
       The school is well-equipped with air-conditioned classrooms, laboratories and School Hall for learning
activities. Under the School Improvement Project, a new wing with a new staff room, 4 classrooms and a staff
common room was built in January 1998. A corner of solitude was founded in the same year to make room for
student activities. In preparation for the New Senior Secondary Curriculum, the Design & Technology Room was
renovated to make room for a second Computer Room for small group and self-access learning activities.

                         Achievements on Major Concern 2 (2022-2023) Annual Report
                                    To Promote a Positive School Culture

The main theme of our school in the academic year (2022-23) was “Positive and Proactive - Thriving on Knowledge,
Confidence and Talent”. In order to promote positive living, a new curriculum of Form Teacher Period was
implemented. A series of activities and schemes were organized to strengthen the class management and encourage
students to embrace challenges.

Objective 1: To promote positive living
Task 1.1 To equip students and teachers with the essential skills to build up positive attitudes and values
⚫ This year, a large variety of training workshops, talks and activities were organized for both students and staff.
     First, two induction programmes and follow-up activity were held successfully for F.1 and F.4 where positive
     attitudes such as compassion, perseverance and respect to others, and team spirit were also built. At the same
     time, talks on peer relationship, positive living and time management respectively for F.2, F.3, F.5 and F.6 at
     the beginning of school year. Extra talks for F.3 on application of character strengths before subject selection
     and F.6 on introduction of guide dogs were also held in the second term in order to let students more understand
     themselves and enhance their positive attributes.
⚫ A staff development programme on communication with parents was conducted by the education psychologist
     from HKU QTN Promoting Wellness Project while another workshop on positive education cum sharing with
     Baptist (Sha Tin Wai) Lui Ming Choi Primary School was arranged by The City University of Hong Kong
     Positive Education Laboratory through the EBD WE Positive Dynamic Scheme. Teachers found it resourceful
     and meaningful.
⚫ Evaluation results showed that over 75% of students agreed that the talks were able to enhance their skills and
     about 71% of students from school-based survey agreed that they were nurtured with positive values. Also,
     around 85% of staff agreed that the development programme was useful.

Task 1.2 To enhance mental health awareness in school through wellness education curriculum
   HKU QTN Promoting Wellness “Out of Dark” Level 1 curriculum was carried out in F.1 Form Periods, Level 2
    curriculum was carried out in F.2 and F.3 Form Periods and Level 3 curriculum was carried out in F.4 and F.5
    Form Periods. The topics covered understanding emotions, emotional management, character strengths,
    information literacy, communication skills, empathy, conflict resolution, gratitude and mental health awareness.
   Evaluation results showed that 77.1% of students from school-based survey agreed that through FP and
    curriculum they could build up positive mindset and skills. Almost 90% of students were satisfied with the
    activities they participated in the first Mental Health Weeks.

Task 1.3 To appreciate and recognize students’ good behaviour and accomplishment and give feedback on their
         performance
   A new award scheme, Good Behaviour Award Scheme, was launched by Guidance and Counselling Team this
    year. The cost of materials was subsidized by HKU QTN project and prizes were subsidized by ILNMHS Team.
    Students showed enthusiasm and actively participated in these activities. Moreover, the regular Student Award

                                                       - P.9 -
     Scheme was coordinated by Careers and Life Planning Team. Over 60 students fulfilled the criteria and won the
     Bronze, Silver and Gold medals. Furthermore, Award for excellent behaviour and commendation from
     Disciplinary Team demonstrated how the school taught the students to appreciate others. Outstanding and
     committed behavior was recognized through service awards and merits given to students.
    Good work from students in different areas such as book reports, calligraphy and art work was displayed on
     board in each classroom and around the school campus.

Task 1.4 To cooperate with Board of Learning and Teaching to educate parents the importance of positive
         reinforcement and recognition of students’ performance
   Parents’ talks and workshops were organized by the Parent-Teacher Association. An online talk for parents about
    study skill with positive reinforcement for children hosted by Vice-Principal was successfully held on 3rd October,
    before the release of the first unified tests. Over 80% of parents agreed that this kind of parent education
    programmes was able to enhance their positive thinking and how to accept and encourage their children.
   A joint school parent talk about National Education for our parents and students was conducted by the Parent-
    Teacher Association on 1st April where some of the positive attributes were involved such as respect for others,
    diligence and appreciation. Also, a talk for the upcoming F.1 parents was organized. The topic was about how to
    support their children to adapt to the new environment and the cooperation with the school.
   The school introduced an award scheme, namely "We Did It!" Award Scheme, to the parents, which was organized
    by the Committee on Home-School Co-operation. The main objective of the scheme is to recognize and reward
    parents who actively support their children's engagement in non-academic school-based or extracurricular
    activities, which can help them develop multiple intelligences and reach their full potential. In the end, 10 families
    nominated were awarded.

Objective 2: To strengthen class management
Task 2.1 To enhance class atmosphere through coordinating class-based or form-based activities aiming at building
          up peer relationship, trust among classmates and teachers, and sense of belonging
    In order to strengthen the cohesion among teachers and students and sense of belonging, varies class-based
     activities were arranged throughout the year. The school grabbed the opportunity of normal school resumption
     and created many meaningful and joyful class activities. The most successful ones are class logo design and
     Good Behaviour Award Scheme. Students showed enthusiasm and actively participated in these activities. In
     addition, the school picnic was rescheduled to a visit to Hong Kong Disneyland in November and a class-based
     photo competition was also held. Both teachers and students enjoyed the visit very much.
    Over 85% of students satisfied with the arrangement for the school picnic and around 70% of students agreed that
     the school provided opportunities to enhance class atmosphere.

Task 2.2 To enhance the effectiveness of class management by regular sharing among Form Teachers and Subject
         Teachers
    Regular meetings for interchange ideas and discussion of student performance among Form Teachers, Vice
     Principal and other related teachers such as those from Disciplinary Team, Guidance Team and Independent
     Learning Needs Support Team were held. Sharing among subject teachers were arranged for lesson
     collaboration and preparation for lesson observation. A focus meeting requested by the HKU for Form Teachers
     was conducted to evaluate the implementation of the QTN Promoting Wellness Curriculum.
    In order to make teachers well prepared for delivering the HKU QTN curriculum, the education psychologist from
     HKU conducted a briefing session for all form teachers and produced teaching videos to support us.
    As a result, Form Teachers’ meetings have been held for at least 4 times.

Objective 3: To encourage students to embrace challenges
Task 3.1 To provide more life-wide learning experiences such as open competitions and special training outside
          school setting for students in order to let them engage more, unleash their potentials and apply their skills
          to face challenges
    A large variety of learning activities were organized. Students were provided opportunities to taste and
     participate in different programmes inside and outside school including serving in Caritas Bazaar, visits to Hong
     Kong Palace Museum and Sing Tao News Corporation Limited, performance of school cultural night, STEM
     and I.T. workshops, leadership training camp, music and art competitions, and voluntary service. Most of the
     charged programmes could be subsidized by Life-wide Learning Grant.
    More than 94% of subject panels and teams successfully nominated and encouraged students to participate in
     open competitions and learning activities outside school. Around 80% of students agreed that the school offered
     a large variety of activities for them.

Task 3.2 To launch a challenge scheme for F.1 students to explore outside their comfort zones
    My Life Planning Portfolio provided by the EDB was introduced to F.1. It aims to help students know better
     about themselves, devise plans for personal development, set and modify goals through self-reflection, and
     grasp various study and career pathways. All F.1 students signed up and participated in the scheme. Besides,
     the Hydroponic Programme to F.1 and F.2 was regularized. Students had to learn the skills of using the new
     technology of planting and monitored the growth of the plants in their classroom. Students were required to set
     up goals and plans at the beginning. During the whole process, responsibility and class cohesion were enhanced.

Task 3.3    To invite stakeholders as a role model to share and demonstrate how they overcome life difficulties and
            face challenges
    In every morning assembly, the Supervisor, the Principal and teachers took turns and shared their life experience
     with all students about how they overcame difficulties. The guest speakers invited to participate in respective

Introduction of Innovation among Teachers: Jockey Club InnoPower Fellowship for Teachers
      3 teachers participated in a project called Jockey Club InnoPower Fellowship for Teachers 1st September 2021
to 31st August 2023. With the funding, overseas training and exposures provided by the Hong Kong Jockey Club,
the 3 teachers explored how students can strengthen their identity as a crucial member in IHMC and part of society's
productive members, through getting students involved with producing food on campus. In the project, students
and teachers formed different interest-based learning circles. Within these circles, teachers and students were equal
co-learners. Teachers no longer facilitated; students were not necessarily the main learners. A phased approach
was adopted allowing students to deepen their knowledge through research, apply that knowledge by producing real
food, reflect and improve upon early attempts, and build community through sharing their final products.
Experiencing the entire process - from researching to producing to reflecting to presenting - transformed isolated
lessons into an innovative learning journey that strengthened students' identities as creative producers within the
IHMC community. Making their own food on campus, students were not only provided experiential education but
also connected more closely to IHMC's history, culture and community. The innovative learning experience
nurtured the growth of citizens proud of their school's past and eager to shape its future.

      The project has had a positive impact according to student observations and feedback. Students' knowledge,
attitudes, behaviors and conditions have changed in desirable ways, indicating InnoPower: Made in IHMC's
effectiveness.

      Most students now understand how the PERMA elements contribute to a flourishing life. They learned more
about food, related environmental and societal issues. Students experienced and cultivated positive emotions like
interest, contentment, love and pride. They are more motivated to participate in school community events involving
parents and alumni. For example, students actively proposed sustaining co-learning circles in coming years with
themes they choose.

                                    Evaluation on the Usage of Various Grants
1. As in previous years, the school made use of specific government funds to recruit extra manpower so that more
   split classes were arranged in the lower forms. Also, a number teaching assistants (including part-time ones)
   were flexibly employed to share the administrative duties of teachers and design teaching materials. They
   offered various types of support to the school and its students.
2. The school made its utmost efforts in utilizing various government funding to subsidize students in learning
   activities. 110 students enjoyed sponsorship in the school picnic held at the Hong Kong Disneyland in
   November 2022. 28 F.1-3 students were subsidized to participate in the Ngong Ping 360 Tai O Culture Tour in
   April 2023. Approximately the same number of students received the grant to join pre-mock examinations in
   various subjects or the “One Music One Dream” Programme. Students were able to broaden their learning
   experiences through these sponsorships.
3. Maximum use of the Life Wide Learning Grant was made to increase students’ exposure and capacity. They
   were used mainly to enhance the students’ performance in various domains. The resources have also facilitated
   students to readjust to school life after full-scale school resumption.
