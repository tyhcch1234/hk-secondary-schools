                                                      Our School
       Immaculate Heart of Mary is a government aided co-educational secondary school founded by the Franciscan
Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows in 1981. The construction of the school campus was completed on 27th
August, 1981 and the official opening and blessing ceremony was held on 9th February, 1983 by The Most Rev.
Bishop John B. Wu, Rev. Sr. M. Francis OSF & Mr. Chan Yan Pang SEO. The school came into existence with 17
teachers and 6 classes of Form 1 and Form 4 respectively. With the support and guidance from the Franciscan
sisters, the school has optimized its class structure in face of changing needs of the society. The current student
population is totaled 766 with 24 classes in F.1 to F6.

                                        A Vision of a Catholic Religious School
       As a staff member of a Catholic school, we should carry out the following core values of Hong Kong Catholic
Education: Truth, Justice, Love, Life and family. We should also follow the Mission and Vision of the School
Sponsoring Body – Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows, and the Guidelines for Franciscan
Education – Go and Teach.
       At the beginning of educational practice, we as Franciscan, share the Church’s commitment to building the
world. We are aware that this social promotion, carried out with the Gospel spirit, is the expression and the sign of
the presence of the Kingdom of God in our society, a work that helps us deepen the educational and anthropological
vision developed by Franciscan Masters.
       In Franciscan spirituality, the concepts of brotherhood and minority constitute the centre or the fundamental
nucleus of interpersonal relationships.
       The fraternity is the vital and proper place of education. It is the place where various values meet and are
shared. The values of acceptance, dialogue, respect for diversity, equality, responsibility, familiarity, trust, happiness,
optimism, peace and forgiveness.
       Minority quantifies and directs relations with others. This value, being the basis for all relations, makes us
simple and obliging, and helps us to move closer to the other with an open heart and ready to discover his inner self.
A style or way of relating which is opposed to any intention of ownership and domination of persons or property and
which prevents any inferiority complex or contempt of itself.
       In the context of fraternity and minority it is important to educate on poverty, solidarity, service to each other,
serious work and building peace.

                                          School Mission Statement
      Immaculate Heart of Mary College is sponsored by the Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows.
Through the example of BLESSED VIRGIN MARY in the LIFE of JESUS, we are led to imitate HER
COMPASSIONATE LOVE and WILLING WORKS of SERVICE TO OTHERS. Empowered by the spirit of
Gospel, we are dedicated to providing a caring and supportive school environment so that learning is valued,
mutual respect is shared and all members can develop their potential to the fullest capacity. By means of our
concern for Catholic Faith, Personal Formation and Scholarly Development, we strive to prepare students to
become responsible citizens who contribute to society, are sensitive to the needs of others and meet the challenges
of today’s world.

                                                     Catholic Faith
      We provide an atmosphere whereby each student is encouraged to seek a relationship with God through generous personal
response to the Gospel and by acquiring an active understanding and experience of the Catholic tradition.


Religious Education and Liturgical Activities
      Since we are a Catholic institute of education, Christian values are to be upheld and exemplified through religious
education. Moreover, students are encouraged to participate daily in prayer, to join the school community in various
liturgical activities, availing themselves of these opportunities to reflect their faith and lifestyle.
       We seek to provide all members of the college, working harmoniously under a common community spirit,
the experience of a genuine sense of belonging, where each one finds charity, support, gentleness, kindness and
tolerance.

                                               Personal Formation
      We aim to develop each student’s value, self-esteem and career through personal guidance in which the
students realize their self-identity, are recognized as individuals, and experience a caring, supportive school
environment.

Holistic Development Approach
      Students are encouraged to reach for excellence in every dimension of their lives (spiritual, moral, cognitive,
emotional, physical, aesthetic and social) through the range of activities offered in the school so that they will grow
towards maturity as persons of character and education.
      Such approach seeks to reveal and develop each student’s character, abilities and leadership potential through
our concern for their religious, emotional, physical, academic and psychological needs.

Career Preparation
      By means of a balanced curriculum, we prepare our students for their own free, informed, conscientious
choices in study, work and life. The essence of our career guidance programs is to assist students to qualify
themselves for further education and careers, and to pursue growth as an individual, capable of independence and
responsible life decisions.

                                            Scholarly Development
      We seek to maximize the achievement opportunities of our students so that they can acquire self-motivation
and independent thinking, respect for knowledge and the search for truth.

Persevering Attitudes
       Students are encouraged to develop their intellectual skills through consistent hard work, regular study habits
and a fulfilling of their duties.

                                                   General Outcomes
      So that each student will live Catholic values, serve humanity and society, and grow towards maturity as a
person of character, of education and of charity, our college aims to develop:
1.    a college community that is genuinely interested in and supportive of all members, fostering individual growth
      and development.
2.    Catholic values, respect for the values of others, and the ability to critically evaluate the current social issues.
3.    an all-round development in moral, spiritual, cognitive, emotional, physical, social and aesthetic education.
4.    an awareness and challenge for students towards their responsibilities to love the less fortunate and the less
      privileged, who hold priority in our care and concern.
5.    the individual interests and abilities of students, for the betterment of their personal welfare and that of society.

                                                Staff Commitment
      Our staff is committed to the total formation of our students through our dedication to the ideals of Catholic
education and of our college, acting as examples for the students and upholding a life-long learning attitude aimed
towards providing quality education to nurture younger generations.

                                                        Our Vision
        Being a professional educational organization which has inherited the education values of the Hong Kong
Province Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Sorrows, we adhere to our school mission to practice the
spirit of the Gospel – loving God and people.
        We envisage that all teaching and administrative staff in the Immaculate Heart of Mary family are dedicated
professionals who keep abreast of the trends in teaching. They are not only role models of students, but also caring
and committed teachers.
        We hope our students will develop simplicity and wholesomeness, while becoming intellectually competent
to learn actively and think independently. Being nurtured to be young leaders who can shoulder responsibility, they
also imitate St. Francis to be a channel of peace in communities.
        We envision that through parent-teacher collaboration and effective utilization of community resources,
students will develop their potential to full capacity. With a sense of social awareness and responsibility, they will be
compassionate in serving others and contributing to communities.

                                                School Facilities
       The school is well-equipped with air-conditioned classrooms, laboratories and School Hall for learning
activities. Under the School Improvement Project, a new wing with a new staff room, 4 classrooms and a staff
common room was built in January 1998. A corner of solitude was founded in the same year to make room for
student activities. In preparation for the New Senior Secondary Curriculum, the Design & Technology Room was
renovated to make room for a second Computer Room for small group and self-access learning activities.

                                           Annual School Report 2024-2025
Major Concern 1 - To foster students' proactive mindset and further enhance self-directed learning capacity
Achievements
All subject panels arranged different programmes and learning activities that align with the school major concern 1 about
supporting students in setting and pursuing meaningful goals for learning and enhancing students’ active engagement and
motivation in learning. Feedbacks were collected from students and teachers.
From students’ survey,
 83% of students can set meaningful goals for learning.
 More than 80% of students applied the skills, such as organization skills and time management skills in their studies
 72.2% of Students have built up learning habits
 77.1% of students did regular review and on-going self-reflection
 69.3% of students showed motivation in their learning.
Reflection
From teachers’ PIE meetings, strengths, areas for improvement and suggested follow-up actions are as follows:
Strengths:
 High engagement in SMART goal setting
 Effective use of learning skills (note-taking, IT tools)
 Proactive student motivation and participation
Areas for Improvement:
 Need for more student autonomy in goal setting
 Superficial self-reflection and lack of follow-up
 Stress and burnout among students
 Inconsistent skill application across subjects
 Tailored support for students with different abilities
Feedback and Follow-up
   Strengthen students’ goal setting skills
   Enhance students’ skills of using different IT tools in their learning and engagement of learning

Major Concern 2: To foster students’ positive mindset and develop a healthy lifestyle
Achievements
Achievements on cultivating students’ growth mindset and healthy lifestyle
1. Promoting Physical and Mental Well-being
   Activities like the Athletic Meet, Inter-House Volleyball Competitions, and the School Picnic provided students
   with opportunities to build resilience, teamwork, and a strong sense of community. The Seasonal Influenza
   Vaccination Outreach Program, HPV vaccination n programme, Fitness Challenge Week and Fruit Day
   emphasized the importance of physical health, while Mental Health Awareness Week offered creative workshops
   such as Zentangle and Stained-Glass Art to enhance emotional resilience. Display boards introducing the
   Growth Mindset and "Ten Sayings of Pope Francis to Youth" were set up to help students understand positive
   values. These activities encouraged students to adopt a healthier lifestyle and mindset.
2. Leadership Development
   Programs like the Leadership Training Camp, Inauguration of Student Leaders, preparation and reception tasks
   for various large-scale events (such as the Athletic Meet, Caritas Bazaar, Graduation Ceremony, Cultural Night)
   allowed student leaders to develop self-confidence, teamwork and problem-solving skills. The Etiquette of
   Prize-Giving Ceremonies Workshop further instilled values of respect, unity, and appreciation of others'
   achievements. These activities empowered students to grow as responsible leaders with a positive outlook.
3. Engaging in Experiential Learning
   Events such as the Happy Green Campaign promoted environmental awareness, while the Summer Job
   Experience Scheme offered students valuable insights into workplace demands, helping them identify strengths
   and areas for improvement. The F.1 Life Planning Workshop, emphasizing dreams and time management,
   inspired younger students to think critically about their future and cultivate a growth mindset. Alumni career
   sharing sessions and interview workshops were conducted to help students deepen their understanding of further
   education and employment knowledge, aiding in their career planning.
Achievements on empowering students to cope with mental health and emotional situations
1. Building Emotional Resilience
   Initiatives like Cheer You Up: Popcorn & Songs Before Exams created a positive and relaxing atmosphere for
   students during stressful periods. The Big Brothers & Big Sisters Activities, including board games and tie-
   tying workshops, fostered peer relationships and emotional support for F.1 students. The "Peer Emotional
   Support Gatekeeper Training" hosted by Hong Kong Children & Youth Services was arranged to take place
   during the Form Teacher Period for F.4 and F.5 students, aimed at enhancing their awareness of mutual support

                                                            - P.6 -
    and teaching methods of emotional support. These activities provided students with tools to manage stress and
    build emotional resilience.
2. Targeted Mental Health Support
   The school implemented Mental Health Support Services, including psychologists and "Happy Friday"
   activities, to address individual needs. Students with mental health challenges received timely intervention,
   while workshops for teachers, such as the Mental Health Workshop, provided strategies for supporting students
   and maintaining their own well-being. We participated in the research project "Survey on Adolescent Balance,
   Attention, and Mental Health Status" hosted by the Department of Rehabilitation Sciences at The Hong Kong
   Polytechnic University, and the "HK Children Happiness and Life Education Index Survey" conducted by
   Lingnan University. This was to further understand the mental health status of students and to utilize the
   resources of tertiary institutions to strengthen cooperation in supporting students' mental and emotional well-
   being. These efforts reflected the school’s commitment to creating a caring environment.
3. Raising Awareness Through Creative Initiatives
    The Stress Ball Design Competition raised awareness of mental health through student-designed logos.
    Activities during Mental Health Awareness Week, like the "Mood Hunt & Claw Machine Challenge,"
    encouraged students to express their emotions and share positivity. During the post exam period, "Sport
    Stacking Activity" was arranged for F.1 to F.3 students to promote their team spirit and enjoyment. These
    programs promoted open conversations about mental health, fostering school-wide understanding.
Feedback and Follow-up
The school found the efforts on Major Concern 2 and the outcomes being satisfactory. The programmes will be
continued in the next school year.

Major Concern 3: To promote values education into students and nurture them into responsible citizens
Achievements
1. Teaching materials of Five Core Values in Catholic and Values Education have been designed and implemented
   during the FP Lesson.
   F.1: Life (Five Core Values in Catholic Education), Perseverance, and Empathy (The twelve priority values and
        attitudes)
   F.2: Truth (Five Core Values in Catholic Education)
   F.3: Family (Five Core Values in Catholic Education), Filial Piety, and Commitment (The twelve priority values
        and attitudes)
   F.4: Justice (Five Core Values in Catholic Education) and Law-abidingness (The twelve priority values and
        attitudes)
   F.5: Love (Five Core Values in Catholic and Respect for Others (The twelve priority values and attitudes)
   F.6: Love (Five Core Values in Catholic Education) and Respect for Others (The twelve priority values and
        attitudes)
   Students exhibited commendable performance in embodying the Catholic core values and the twelve priority
   values and attitudes. Most students were able to apply positive values and mention the need for correct behavior
   through reflecting on issues, including how to interact with family members to demonstrate filial piety, and the
   qualities and conditions that students should possess as school leaders.
2. We strengthened national education through learning activities, such as book exhibitions on Victory Day of
    Chinese People’s War of Resistance against Japanese Aggression National Day exhibition, Territory-wide Inter-
    school National Security Knowledge Challenge, visit to the National Security Exhibition Gallery, Territory-
    wide Inter-school National Security Knowledge Challenge, etc. Our students possessed a very good value and
    attitude towards national and global identity, for example, a sense of national identity in loving the Chinese
    culture, respect for people from different cultural backgrounds, etc. They showed good attitudes in various
    ceremonies, activities and competitions such as flag-raising ceremonies, territory-wide inter-school national
    security competitions, etc. For example, 100% of students participated in territory-wide inter-school
    competitions (KPM21) for two consecutive years. The results of APASO indicates that the Q-value for our
    students regarding national identity (including responsibilities, obligations, pride, care, national flag, national
    anthem and national achievements) is 116, which is higher than the territory-wide level.
    Five F.5 students successfully advanced to the finals and achieved third place in the inter-school team
    competition of the Territory-wide Inter-school National Security Knowledge Challenge.

    values and attitudes. Most students are able to apply positive values and mention the need for correct behavior
    through reflecting on issues, including how to interact with family members to demonstrate filial piety and the
    qualities and conditions that students should possess as school leaders.

(3) Student Performance
Student’ Attitude and Behaviour
Our students exhibit comprehensive and balanced performance in various aspects including spirituality, morality,
intellect, physical health, social skills, and aesthetics. They are engaged in various school activities to groom
themselves into future leaders with high moral standards.

Our students also manage to establish harmonious relationships with teachers, striving to develop correct values and
self-awareness, understanding their uniqueness, and fostering a caring and encouraging atmosphere within the school.

Furthermore, our students actively participate in community services, embodying the spirit of Catholic education.
They courageously serve the community, becoming well-rounded individuals. They care for the vulnerable, visiting
low-income families, the homeless, and solitary elderly, bringing Christ's love to them.

   split classes were arranged in the lower forms. Also, a number teaching assistants (including part-time ones)
   were flexibly employed to share the administrative duties of teachers and design teaching materials. They

3. Maximum use of the Life Wide Learning Grant was made to increase students’ exposure and capacity. They
